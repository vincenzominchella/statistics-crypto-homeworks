Bernoulli Increments Xi ∈ {0, 1} Cumulative breach count. Each node breached (+1) or resisted (0). Total breaches diverge as n · p; average breach rate converges to p. Sample variance stabilizes; distribution follows Binomial → Gaussian.
Rademacher Increments Xi ∈ {-1, +1} Dynamic defense-attacker competition (+1 exploit, -1 automated quarantine). Balanced zero-mean walk; random excursions scale with √n. Empirical endpoint density converges cleanly to standard normal 𝒩(0, 1).
Worst-Case Penetration Outlier maxj Sn(j) The “Adversary Swarm Advantage”. Even if mean μ = 0, outliers breach deep. Worst-case breach depth scales as √(2n ln m) by extreme value theory. More attackers increase the probability of at least one catastrophic breach.
